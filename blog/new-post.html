Write a post

Publishing as Kaya Ellery. Posts appear in the blog index immediately.

The post

Between 10 and 90 characters. Say what the reader will learn.

One or two sentences, shown on the preview card in the index.

Plain text. Use a blank line between paragraphs. Tutorials read better with short sections and a heading each.

Header image
JPEG or PNG, up to 4 MB. Landscape images crop better on the index cards.

Describe what the image shows, for readers using a screen reader. Required whenever an image is attached.

Publishing

Leave as today to publish now.

Publish post Cancel

Writing a good tutorial

  • Say what the reader will be able to do at the end.
  • One technique per post. Split anything longer.
  • Photograph in daylight, and show the failure as well as the success.
  • Name the exact paints and brushes you used.

Image descriptions are required

Every attached image needs a description. It is what readers using a screen reader get instead of the picture, and it is also what appears if the image fails to load.
