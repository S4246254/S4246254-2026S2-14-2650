Oxide Wash does one job and does it properly

A review of Oxide Wash, 30 ml.

★★★★★★★★★★ 4.5 out of 5 Verified purchase

Oksana Reyes Written 28 July 2026 Bought 9 July 2026

I bought two pots to get through a squad of eight vehicles, and I have used about a pot and a half. That is a fair test, so here is what it actually does.

Flow

This is the part that matters and the part cheaper washes get wrong. It runs into panel lines and rivet recesses and stays there, without pooling in the middle of a flat plate and drying as a tidemark. On a hull side I could apply it, walk away, and come back to clean separation rather than a stained panel.

Drying time

About twenty minutes to touch-dry in a warm room, and closer to an hour before I would drybrush over it. That is slower than I expected, and it is the reason this is not a five-star review — batching eight vehicles means a lot of standing around unless you plan the order of work.

Over a gloss coat

Considerably better over gloss than over matt, which is worth knowing before you start. On a matt surface it grips slightly and you get a faint halo around the recess. A quick gloss coat first and that goes away entirely.

Would I buy it again

Yes, and I have. The only reason it loses half a star is the drying time. If you are shading bare metal or weathered plate this is the one I would reach for.
