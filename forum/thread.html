Is the Sentinel actually worth its points at 1500?

14 replies · 612 views · last active 13 August 2026

Reply to thread

Opening post

Two walkers or a third infantry block?

Opening post

Toma Brandt 10 August 2026 at 8:22 am

I have run the Sentinel in every list since the points update and I am no longer convinced. At 210 points it is close to a third infantry block, and the block scores objectives. The walker does not.

Its gun is genuinely good — a reliable long-range shot that ignores light cover. But it only fires once per turn, and the moment anything closes to within twelve inches it is a very expensive shield-bearer that cannot hold ground.

Convince me I am wrong. Specifically: what is it doing in the mid-game that a block of infantry with the same points is not doing better?

Reply Posted by Toma Brandt

    Your post

    Kaya Ellery 10 August 2026 at 10:05 am

    You are measuring it against objectives, which it was never going to win. Measure it against what your opponent does on turn one instead. In six of my last eight games the Sentinel ate the entire first round of anti-armour shooting, because leaving it alive is worse than shooting anything else.

    That is 210 points buying a turn of near-total safety for your infantry. The block you want to add instead would be taking those hits, and blocks fold when they lose a third of their models.
